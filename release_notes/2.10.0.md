## English

On Linux, a slide with a background video no longer freezes the app. The fix applies to the `.deb`, the `.rpm`, and the AppImage, on Ubuntu 22.04 or newer and on recent Fedora. On those versions, the AppImage window is drawn again as well.

When an update is available, “See what's new” opens these notes in the language selected in Salmia.
